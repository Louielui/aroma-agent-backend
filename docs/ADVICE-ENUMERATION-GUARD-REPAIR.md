## Bounded repair

The existing guard admits a locally verifiable enumeration only on the host-reviewed no-read advice path. It requires a documentation obligation, an explicit count from two to sixteen, exactly that many distinct open questions separated by the list delimiter, and no known business entity, current/company referent, status predicate or nested quantity. Chinese numerals reuse the existing answer-plan parser. Inherited operational subjects cannot gain this admission.

Ambiguous prose, mismatched or duplicated items, factual items and current/company-specific requirements retain the original evidence checks. Counts and status assertions in adjacent clauses or sentences remain checked. No entity vocabulary, read authority, model tool, approval rule, subscription route or usage budget changes. This recognizes a bounded grammatical structure, not a particular saved sentence or a general advice exemption.

This intentionally does not recognize every possible enumeration or language. The shared guard's existing vocabulary and nonnumeric semantic blind spots remain. The current native output is evidence of a failure, never relabelled as a pass. Offline replay can prove deterministic repair and wiring; it cannot prove a new model response will satisfy the overall goal.

Self-review found that Unicode letter matching also admitted Chinese numerals inside an item or a quantified actor. A failing negative control confirmed both variants. Admission now requires numeral-free actor and item text; this is deliberately stricter than the existing business measure-word parser, whose vocabulary omits some units. A presentation-path test also required recognizing an unordered Markdown marker without altering the source bytes.
